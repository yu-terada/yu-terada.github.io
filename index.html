About Me

I am a Project Lecturer at the Center for AI and Data Science, Ochanomizu University.

I am interested in information processing and dynamics in neuronal systems. I use theories from physics and mathematics to address problems in neuroscience.

Previously, I was a researcher at Sony Group Corporation and held research positions at the University of California, San Diego, RIKEN Center for Brain Science, and Tokyo Institute of Technology.

I received my Doctor of Informatics from Kyoto University in 2017, supervised by Prof. Toshio Aoyagi.

Fields of Interest

  • Theoretical (Computational) Neuroscience
  • Machine Learning
  • Statistical Physics

Nonlinear dynamics, phase oscillators, neural networks, statistical inference, chaos, computation.
